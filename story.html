Our Story

From grandmother's kitchen to your family table

The Beginning

In 1987, in a small village in Tamil Nadu, Kamala Devi noticed her grandchildren struggling with modern dietary challenges. Despite having access to more food than ever before, they lacked the sustained energy and robust health that previous generations enjoyed.

As a traditional healer and keeper of ancestral recipes, Kamala Devi spent months perfecting a blend of 36+ natural ingredients - each chosen for its specific nutritional and medicinal properties. This wasn't just food; it was medicine disguised as nourishment.

The Legacy Grows

Word spread quickly through the village. Families noticed remarkable changes: children had more energy for studies, elders felt stronger, and digestive issues that had plagued households for years began to disappear. The secret was in the perfect balance of ancient grains, healing spices, and nutrient-dense ingredients.

For over three decades, this precious recipe remained within our family, passed down through generations and refined with each passing year. Every ingredient was sourced locally, every batch prepared with the same care and attention that Kamala Devi had insisted upon.

Bringing Tradition to Modern Families

In 2020, as the world faced unprecedented health challenges, we realized that Kamala Devi's wisdom was needed more than ever. Modern families were struggling with processed foods, nutritional deficiencies, and lifestyle diseases that our ancestors rarely faced.

That's when Aurawill was born - not as a business venture, but as a mission to share the healing power of traditional nutrition with every Indian family. We partnered with local farmers, ensuring that every ingredient meets the same quality standards that Kamala Devi would have demanded.
