Delicious Ways to Enjoy Aurawill

Transform your daily nutrition with these easy, tasty recipes using Aurawill Health Mix

Health Mix Drink

⏱️ 5 mins 👥 1 serving

A nutritious drink perfect for breakfast or evening snack.

  1. Boil 1 cup milk or water
  2. Add 2 scoops Aurawill mix
  3. Stir well to avoid lumps
  4. Add jaggery or honey to taste
  5. Serve hot and enjoy

Health Mix Dosa

⏱️ 15 mins 👥 2 servings

Crispy and nutritious dosa for a healthy meal.

  1. Mix 1 cup Aurawill with water
  2. Add salt and chopped onions
  3. Make a smooth batter
  4. Cook on hot tawa like regular dosa
  5. Serve with chutney

Health Mix Chapati

⏱️ 20 mins 👥 3 servings

Soft and nutritious chapati for daily meals.

  1. Mix Aurawill with wheat flour (1:1)
  2. Add water gradually to make dough
  3. Knead well and rest for 10 mins
  4. Roll and cook on tawa
  5. Serve with curry or dal

Health Mix Ladoo

⏱️ 25 mins 👥 4 servings

Sweet and healthy ladoos for festivals or treats.

  1. Dry roast Aurawill mix until fragrant
  2. Add melted ghee and mix well
  3. Add powdered jaggery
  4. Mix and shape into ladoos
  5. Cool and store in airtight container

Health Mix Smoothie

⏱️ 3 mins 👥 1 serving

Refreshing smoothie packed with nutrition.

  1. Blend 2 scoops Aurawill with banana
  2. Add 1 cup cold milk or yogurt
  3. Add honey and ice cubes
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Serve chilled

Health Mix Pancakes

⏱️ 15 mins 👥 2 servings

Fluffy pancakes for a healthy breakfast.

  1. Mix 1 cup Aurawill with 1 egg
  2. Add milk to make batter
  3. Add pinch of baking powder
  4. Cook on non-stick pan
  5. Serve with fruits and honey

Health Mix Cookies

⏱️ 30 mins 👥 6 servings

Crunchy cookies perfect for tea time.

  1. Mix 2 cups Aurawill with ghee
  2. Add jaggery powder and cardamom
  3. Make dough and shape cookies
  4. Bake at 180°C for 15 mins
  5. Cool and store

Health Mix Paratha

⏱️ 25 mins 👥 3 servings

Stuffed parathas with health mix filling.

  1. Make dough with wheat flour
  2. Prepare filling with Aurawill mix
  3. Add spices and vegetables
  4. Stuff and roll parathas
  5. Cook with ghee until golden

Cooking Tips

🥛 Perfect Consistency

Always add liquid gradually to avoid lumps. Stir continuously for smooth texture.

🍯 Natural Sweetening

Use jaggery, honey, or dates for natural sweetness instead of refined sugar.

🔥 Roasting Enhances Flavor

Dry roast Aurawill mix for 2-3 minutes to enhance nutty flavor in recipes.

❄️ Storage Tips

Store prepared items in airtight containers. Refrigerate smoothies and drinks.
