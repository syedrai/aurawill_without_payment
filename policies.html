Refund Policy

Return & Refund Eligibility

We want you to be completely satisfied with your Aurawill Health Mix purchase. If you're not satisfied, we offer a hassle-free refund policy:

  • 30-Day Money Back Guarantee: If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you can return it within 30 days from the date of delivery for a full refund.
  • Unopened Products: Unopened, unused products in original packaging are eligible for full refund or exchange.
  • Opened Products: We accept opened products if there is a quality or defect issue. A minimum of 50% product remaining is required for consideration.
  • Damaged Shipments: Products damaged during transit will be replaced immediately or refunded in full.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Contact us at refunds@aurawill.com or call +91 98438 11708
  2. Provide your Order ID and reason for return
  3. Ship the product back to us (prepaid shipping label will be provided)
  4. We'll inspect and process your refund within 5-7 business days after receipt
  5. Refund will be credited to your original payment method

Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned product. Please allow an additional 3-5 business days for the amount to reflect in your account depending on your bank.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Products purchased more than 30 days ago
  • Products that show signs of tampering or contamination
  • Products not in original packaging (for opened products without quality issues)

Exchanges

If you received a defective product or would prefer a different size, we'll exchange it for free. Simply contact us with your Order ID.

Shipping Policy

Delivery Coverage

We deliver to all major cities and towns across India through our trusted logistics partners.

  • Pan-India Delivery: We ship to all states and union territories
  • Metro Cities: Delivery within 2-3 business days
  • Non-Metro Areas: Delivery within 5-7 business days
  • Remote Locations: Delivery within 10-14 business days

Shipping Charges

  • Orders below ₹500: Free shipping on first order, ₹50 for subsequent orders
  • Orders ₹500 and above: FREE shipping across India
  • Bulk Orders (5+ units): Special discounts available. Contact us for quotes.

Order Processing

Processing Time: Orders are processed within 24 hours of placement (excluding weekends and national holidays). You'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS once your order is dispatched.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you'll receive:

  • Tracking number via email and SMS
  • Real-time tracking updates
  • Estimated delivery date
  • Delivery partner contact details

Delivery Address Guidelines

Please ensure:

  • Complete and accurate address with PIN code
  • Correct phone number for delivery coordination
  • Clear building/house name and number
  • Nearby landmark if available

What If I'm Not Home?

Our delivery partners will attempt delivery 3 times. If unsuccessful, we'll reschedule or arrange for pickup at the nearest collection center. You can always coordinate with them using the tracking number.

Damage or Loss During Transit

In case of damage or loss:

  1. Do NOT accept the damaged package at delivery
  2. Contact us immediately with photos at support@aurawill.com
  3. We'll arrange replacement or refund within 48 hours
